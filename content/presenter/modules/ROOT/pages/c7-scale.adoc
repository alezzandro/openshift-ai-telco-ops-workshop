= C7. Horizontal vs vertical scale

Time: about 10 minutes.

== Definitions (say them this way)

* **Vertical** — more GPUs *inside one replica* (tensor parallel). You just did this.
* **Horizontal** — more *replicas* of a model that **already fits** on its GPU set. Throughput and availability, not extra VRAM for a bigger model.

You cannot horizontal-scale a 4-GPU tensor-parallel service on a single 4-GPU node: one replica already consumed the node.

== Click path

. Delete the 2-GPU Phi-4 **InferenceService** (or stop it). Keep the Model Registry entries.
. **AI hub → Registry** → **workshop** → the Ministral (or Qwen) version from C3/C4 → ⋮ → **Deploy** onto *NVIDIA L4 - 1 GPU*. Do not go back to the catalog **Deploy** button.
. Set min replicas 1 and max replicas 2 (dashboard deployment / scaling fields).
. Show two pods, two GPUs.

Optional: try a third replica and watch it **queue** in Kueue or stay Pending (only 4 GPUs; other work may still hold cards).
